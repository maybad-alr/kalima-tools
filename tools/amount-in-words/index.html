Frequently asked questions

What is Tafqeet (تفقيط)?

Tafqeet is the Arabic legal convention of writing the monetary amount in words on a financial document so that the numeric figure cannot be altered. Any Arabic invoice template or cheque asks for it.

Does it follow correct Arabic grammar?

Yes — dual forms (ريالان), the 3-10 plural rules, the special teens (أحد عشر, اثنا عشر) and subunits (halala, fils, piastre, baisa) are all handled per currency.

Is it free and private?

Yes. The converter runs entirely in your browser; no amount or document ever leaves your device.
