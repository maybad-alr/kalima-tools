Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Arabic and Arabic-Indic numerals?

“Arabic numerals” (0-9) are the ones English uses. “Arabic-Indic numerals” (٠١٢) originated in India and are used in Arabic-language documents, printed books and the Arabic-locale settings of phones.

Does it change digits inside URLs or code?

It converts every digit you feed it, so paste URLs or code at your own discretion — designers normally only paste prose.

Which countries use ٠١٢ vs 012?

Arabic-Indic ٠١٢ is standard in printed Arabic across the Gulf, Egypt and the Levant; North Africa and daily digital life mostly use 012.
