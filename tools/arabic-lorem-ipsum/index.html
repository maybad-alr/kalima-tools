Frequently asked questions

Why not just paste Latin lorem?

Arabic script connects, has different average word length, and renders right-to-left — Latin filler hides layout bugs that only appear in RTL scripts.

Is the text real Arabic?

It is grammatically shaped filler built from real UI-related words — readable as texture, meaningless as prose, exactly like classic lorem.
