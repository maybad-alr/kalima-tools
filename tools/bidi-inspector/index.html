Frequently asked questions

What is the RLO character?

Right-to-Left Override (U+202E) forces everything after it to render reversed — used in pranks, and it can even hide filenames. This tool always flags it as high risk.

Is ZWNJ safe to keep?

Yes — the zero-width non-joiner (used in Persian and in emoji sequences) is functional, not directional, so the cleaner preserves it.

Why do my Arabic strings look fine in Word but break on the web?

Apps and browsers apply different bidi resolution defaults and sanitize different character subsets — the marks exist in the clipboard and only misbehave in some renderers.
